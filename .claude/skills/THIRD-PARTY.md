## Precedence

`art-gallery-website` is this project's own brief. Where these skills disagree with it on palette,
type, motion, layout or house rules (borders instead of shadows, no gradients, no glassmorphism,
no bouncing), `art-gallery-website` wins. Use these skills for UX checklists, accessibility rules,
interaction patterns and anything the gallery brief does not cover.
